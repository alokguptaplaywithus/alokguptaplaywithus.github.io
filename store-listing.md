## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Take command of your starship and defend the galaxy in SPACE BLASTER, a fast, addictive arcade shooter made for quick sessions and high scores.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag your finger to steer. Your ship fires automatically.
• Dodge enemy fire and destroy waves of alien ships.
• Defeat a giant boss at the end of every level.

FEATURES
• Simple one-finger controls
• Three enemy types: scouts, armored tanks and fast zigzag raiders
• Epic boss battles that get harder each level
• Power-ups: multi-shot, energy shield and extra lives
• Glowing neon visuals and retro sound effects
• Beat your own best score
• Works offline, no internet needed
• Lightweight and smooth on any phone

How far can you get? Download Space Blaster and find out!
